Contact EduSow

Questions, feedback, or a problem with a study tool? We'd like to hear about it.

Support and feedback

You can contact the project through the EduSow GitHub Issues page.

When reporting a problem, describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and which device/browser you use. Please do not post passwords, private study documents, personal records, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

AI-generated content

If a generated quiz or flashcard contains an error, include a short example without sharing copyrighted or private material, and explain what needs correcting.

GitHub issues may be publicly visible. Do not include private contact details or sensitive information in a public report.
