EduSow Help Centre

Make the most of your study time

New to EduSow? Follow these quick guides to turn your study materials into lessons, practise what you learn, and build a steady study routine.

🌐 Read this page in your language

1

📄 Study Materials & File Extraction

Use your own notes or textbook pages to start learning.

  1. Open Study Materials on the homepage.
  2. Choose a PDF, JPG, or PNG file from your device.
  3. Select the extraction option and wait for the text to appear.
  4. Check the extracted text for mistakes, especially if the page is blurry or scanned.
2

🧠 Explain Materials

Get help understanding the material, not just memorising it.

  1. Extract text from your file first.
  2. Open the explanation option and choose your preferred language when asked.
  3. Follow the lesson slides and use the teacher or follow-up question features when available.
  4. If the result looks incorrect, check the extracted text and try again.
3

❓ Generate a Quiz

Test your understanding with questions based on your study material.

  1. Extract your study text first.
  2. Go to the Quiz section and choose the output language if an option is shown.
  3. Generate the questions, answer them yourself, then review your results.
  4. Use mistakes as a guide for what to revise next.
4

🗂️ Flashcards

Practise key terms, facts, and concepts with active recall.

  1. Extract text from your file.
  2. Open Flashcards and select your preferred language if available.
  3. Generate cards, try to answer before revealing the back, and repeat the difficult ones.
5

🍅 Pomodoro & Study Tracker

Build focus sessions and keep track of your study time.

  1. Open Pomodoro on the homepage.
  2. Choose Stopwatch for elapsed time or Pomodoro for focus and break cycles.
  3. Set the focus and break lengths if using Pomodoro.
  4. Press Start studying. Pause when needed, and finish the session to save it in your study history on this device.
6

💡 Tips for better results

  • Use clear, readable files and include only the pages you need.
  • Always check AI-generated explanations, answers, and quizzes against your learning materials.
  • Try explaining a topic in your own words after studying it.
  • For better memory, revisit difficult flashcards and quiz questions later.

Something isn't working?

Try refreshing the page, choosing a clearer file, and extracting the text again. AI-generated content can sometimes be incomplete or incorrect, so review it before relying on it for schoolwork. Study history is saved in your browser on this device, so it may not appear on another device or browser.
