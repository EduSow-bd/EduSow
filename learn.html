How to Study Effectively: 7 Evidence-Based Techniques for Better Learning

Studying for hours does not always mean learning effectively. The way you practise, review, and test your understanding can make a real difference. This guide explains seven practical techniques you can use in school, university, or independent learning.

The central idea: Do more than reread your notes. Try to remember information without looking, return to it over several days, and use it to solve new problems.

1. Use active recall

Active recall means trying to retrieve what you have learned from memory instead of immediately looking at the answer. After reading a section, close the book and write down its main ideas. Then reopen the book and check what you missed.

For example, after learning the causes of evaporation, explain the process without looking at your science notes. If you cannot explain one part, review it and try again. The aim is not to prove that you already know everything; it is to discover what you can and cannot yet recall.

Try this: Read a short section, close the material, and write five questions about it. Answer from memory, then check your answers and correct gaps.

Active recall হলো বই বন্ধ করে নিজের স্মৃতি থেকে শেখা বিষয় মনে করার চেষ্টা করা। আগে উত্তর দেখবে না। পরে বই খুলে ভুলগুলো যাচাই করবে এবং যে অংশ মনে পড়েনি, সেটি আবার বুঝে নেবে।

2. Space out your revision

Spaced practice means reviewing information across separate study sessions instead of doing all the revision at once. Returning to a topic on later days gives you repeated opportunities to retrieve and strengthen your knowledge.

You might review a new topic on the day you learn it, revisit it the next day, test yourself several days later, and review it again the following week. These intervals are an example, not a universal formula. Make reviews closer together when the material is difficult or you keep forgetting it.

Example: If you learn 12 English words on Monday, test yourself on Tuesday, Friday, and the following Monday. Spend extra time on words you keep forgetting.
বাংলায় সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

Spaced practice মানে একদিনে বারবার পড়ার বদলে কয়েক দিন পরপর একই বিষয় অনুশীলন করা। যে অংশ ভুলে যাও, সেটি আরও ঘন ঘন ঝালাই করো।

3. Test yourself and learn from mistakes

Practice questions help you discover the difference between what you think you know and what you can actually recall or solve. Use questions, flashcards, short written answers, and worked problems. After attempting them, check the answers and understand why each mistake occurred.

Do not treat a score as the only goal. A wrong answer can be useful when it reveals a misunderstanding you can correct before an examination.

Keep a small error log: Write down the question, your mistake, the correct reasoning, and one similar question to solve later. Revisit the log before your next practice test.
বাংলায় সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

নিজেকে প্রশ্ন করা তোমার দুর্বলতা খুঁজে বের করতে সাহায্য করে। ভুল উত্তর পেলে শুধু সঠিক উত্তর মুখস্থ কোরো না; কেন ভুল হয়েছে তা বোঝো এবং আবার অনুশীলন করো।

4. Mix related problem types

Interleaving means practising more than one related type of problem in a study session. Instead of solving twenty identical questions, you might alternate between percentages, ratios, and fractions after learning how each method works.

This encourages you to decide which method fits each question. If a topic is completely new, first work through enough examples to understand the basic method. Mixing problems is most useful once you can attempt each type.

বাংলায় সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

Interleaving মানে সম্পর্কিত বিভিন্ন ধরনের অঙ্ক মিশিয়ে অনুশীলন করা। এতে প্রশ্ন দেখে কোন নিয়মটি ব্যবহার করতে হবে, তা চিনতে শেখা যায়। তবে প্রথমে প্রতিটি নিয়মের ভিত্তি ভালোভাবে বুঝে নাও।

5. Explain ideas in your own words

After studying a concept, explain it as if you were teaching someone who has never learned it. Use simple language, give an example, and identify any step you cannot explain clearly. Return to the source to resolve that gap.

This can be useful for science, history, mathematics, and reading comprehension. For a mathematical rule, explain not only how to use it but also why it works. If your explanation depends on repeating the textbook wording without understanding it, try a simpler example.

বাংলায় সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

কোনো বিষয় নিজের ভাষায় এমনভাবে বোঝাও, যেন তুমি অন্য কাউকে শেখাচ্ছ। যে অংশটি পরিষ্কারভাবে বোঝাতে পারবে না, সেটি আবার পড়ে বোঝার চেষ্টা করো।

6. Connect ideas to concrete examples

Abstract definitions can be difficult to remember when they have no clear meaning to you. Connect them to everyday life, diagrams, small experiments, or problems you already understand.

For example, learn percentages by calculating a discount. If a product costs 500 units of currency and has a 10% discount, the discount is 500 × 10 ÷ 100 = 50 units. The final price is 450 units. After understanding the example, solve a different problem without copying the same numbers.

বাংলায় সহজ ব্যাখ্যা

কঠিন ধারণাকে দৈনন্দিন জীবনের উদাহরণের সঙ্গে মিলিয়ে শেখো। যেমন শতকরা হিসাব বুঝতে কেনাকাটার ছাড়ের উদাহরণ ব্যবহার করতে পারো। এরপর নতুন অঙ্ক নিজে সমাধান করো।

7. Use helpful visuals

A well-labelled diagram, timeline, flowchart, or comparison table can help explain relationships that are difficult to follow in a long paragraph. Use visuals that clarify the material, not decorations that distract you.

For example, use a labelled diagram to learn the parts of a plant, a timeline to organise historical events, or a flowchart to understand an algorithm. Explain what each visual means in your own words.

প্রয়োজন অনুযায়ী চিত্র, সময়রেখা, ফ্লোচার্ট বা ছক ব্যবহার করো। শুধু সুন্দর দেখানোর জন্য ছবি যোগ না করে, ছবিটি কীভাবে বিষয়টি বুঝতে সাহায্য করে তা নিশ্চিত করো।

A practical seven-day study plan

Use this example to organise one topic. It is a starting point, not a strict scientific formula. Adjust the workload and review intervals to your subject and needs.

DayWhat to do
Day 1Learn the topic, understand examples, and write questions.
Day 2Recall the main ideas without notes; correct mistakes.
Day 3Solve practice questions and explain difficult concepts.
Day 4Review weak points and use flashcards for key facts.
Day 5Mix related question types and explain your reasoning.
Day 6Take a short closed-book practice test.
Day 7Review errors and identify what needs more practice.

প্রথম দিন বিষয়টি বোঝো। পরের দিনগুলোতে বই বন্ধ করে মনে করো, প্রশ্ন সমাধান করো, ভুল যাচাই করো এবং দুর্বল অংশ আবার অনুশীলন করো। সপ্তম দিনে নিজেকে পরীক্ষা করে পরবর্তী কাজ ঠিক করো।

How to use these methods in a normal study session

  1. Set a clear goal. Decide exactly what you want to learn.
  2. Study with attention. Read an explanation and work through an example.
  3. Recall without looking. Write or say what you remember.
  4. Practise. Answer questions and solve problems independently.
  5. Check and correct. Understand errors instead of simply memorising answers.
  6. Schedule a review. Return to the topic later and test yourself again.

You can use a Pomodoro timer to organise focused work and breaks. The timer helps structure the session; it does not automatically make learning effective. The quality of your practice matters.

Frequently asked questions

Is rereading completely useless?

No. Reading is useful for first understanding a topic and checking details. The problem is relying on rereading alone without testing what you can remember or apply.

How long should a study session last?

There is no single duration that suits everyone. Choose a manageable block of focused work, take breaks when needed, and adjust the schedule to the task and your attention.

Should I use all seven techniques every day?

No. Start with active recall, spaced revision, and feedback from practice questions. Add the other methods when they fit the material you are learning.

What if I keep forgetting what I study?

Try shorter retrieval attempts, review the material over several days, check your mistakes, and revisit difficult ideas. Forgetting does not mean you cannot learn; it shows where more practice may be needed.
