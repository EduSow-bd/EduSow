About EduSow

Learn. Explore. Grow.

EduSow is a learning-focused website designed to help students study more actively and organize their study time.

What you can do here

  • Study-material text extraction: extract readable text from supported PDF, JPG, and PNG files.
  • AI-generated quizzes: turn extracted study text into practice questions.
  • Flashcards: create question-and-answer cards for revision.
  • Pomodoro and study tracking: focus in sessions and review study-time statistics saved in your browser.

Our approach

EduSow aims to make study tools accessible and easy to use. AI-generated material can contain mistakes, so students should check answers against their textbooks, teachers, and trusted learning resources.

EduSow is a study aid. It does not replace teachers, official textbooks, or school guidance.
