About EduSow

Learn. Explore. Grow.

EduSow is a student-built educational platform created to help students study more actively, stay organized, and develop better learning habits. Our goal is to make useful study tools more accessible and engaging.

What EduSow Offers

  • PDF and image text extraction: Extract readable text from supported study documents.
  • Explain Materials: Turn extracted study material into AI-generated interactive lesson slides or teacher-style explanations in English or Bangla.
  • AI-generated quizzes: Turn extracted study material into practice questions.
  • Flashcards: Review questions and answers to support revision.
  • Pomodoro and study tracking: Organize focus sessions and review study-time statistics saved in the browser.

Our Mission

We believe learning should be active, accessible, and rewarding. EduSow aims to give students practical tools for independent study, regular practice, and better time management.

Accuracy and Responsible Learning

AI-generated explanations, quizzes, and flashcards may contain mistakes. Students should check important information against their textbooks, teachers, and trusted educational resources.

EduSow is a study aid. It does not replace teachers, official textbooks, or school guidance.
