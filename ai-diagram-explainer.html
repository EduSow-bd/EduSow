Understand Study Diagrams with an AI Diagram Explainer

A difficult diagram can make an otherwise understandable topic feel confusing. EduSow's Explain Materials feature uses AI to examine visual content in your study material and turn it into a guided lesson.

What can it help explain?

When you upload supported study material, EduSow can analyse visual elements such as:

  • Diagrams and labelled illustrations: identify visible parts and explain how they relate to one another.
  • Charts and graphs: interpret titles, axes, labels, trends and comparisons.
  • Maps: explain visible labels, regions, symbols and the information represented.
  • Flowcharts: follow the visible steps, arrows and connections.
  • Scientific and geometry figures: explain the visible components, markings and relationships.

How visual explanation works

  1. Open EduSow and go to Study Materials.
  2. Upload a PDF or a supported image containing the diagram or illustration.
  3. Open Explain Materials to generate a guided AI lesson.
  4. Study the explanation and ask follow-up questions about confusing parts.

The aim is to explain the visual information in context, rather than simply extract the words printed around it.

Can AI explain every diagram perfectly?

No AI system can guarantee a perfect interpretation of every image. Very small labels, blurry scans, low contrast, unusual symbols or incomplete diagrams can affect the result. Check important details against your textbook or teacher's explanation, especially when a label is unclear.

Who can use the AI diagram explainer?

Students who learn from textbook figures, science illustrations, geography maps, mathematical graphs, process diagrams and other visual study materials can use this feature as a learning aid.

Frequently asked questions

Can EduSow explain diagrams inside a PDF?

Yes. Explain Materials can analyse selected visual pages from supported PDFs, subject to image and processing limits.

Can it explain charts and graphs?

It is designed to interpret visible chart and graph elements, including labels, axes, trends and comparisons, when they are clear enough to read.

Can I ask questions after the explanation?

Yes. You can ask follow-up questions about the lesson and the material being studied.

Does it work with image files?

Explain Materials supports supported image uploads as well as PDF study materials.
